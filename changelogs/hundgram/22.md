- [Predictive back](tg://settings/inu/predictive-back-mode): MD3 animations for bottom sheets, photo viewer and stories, enabled by default on supported devices, closes an open drawer
- Material 3 drawer: pill rows, rounded edge, inline account list, floating theme toggle
- Hidden messages are never hidden inside the peer's own chat, and keep zero-height rows so reply jumps land on target
- Classic UI: no fade views, sheet bottom bar stays in place after keyboard transitions
- Update bar says Hundgram again
- Launcher widget descriptions and the system Accounts settings entry show their text again
